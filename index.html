Pupil Response Test

Your front camera tracks your irises and estimates pupil size while each item appears. Each item shows as a picture. Before it you see a scrambled version of the same picture, with the same brightness, as the baseline. Then you rate the item. At the end you'll see whether your pupils got bigger for things you like.

For a usable result: sit in steady, even light (no window behind you, no lamp moving). Hold the phone at about 30 cm, eyes level with the camera. Set brightness to a fixed level and turn off auto-brightness and Night Shift / True Tone. Don't blink during items. In the eye previews, the pupil should show as a red-filled dark area. If it doesn't, move to brighter light.

Results

"Δ pupil" is the change in pupil size during the item compared with the grey baseline just before it. Positive means dilation. "Quality" is the share of frames where both pupils were measured. Rows below 60% are unreliable. Phone-camera pupillometry is noisy: treat this as a fun experiment, not a lie detector. Run it 2–3 times and see whether the pattern holds.
